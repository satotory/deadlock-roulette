HOW CHANCE WORKS

Fair roulette.
Your rules.

Every item receives its tier weight. Its chance is its weight divided by the sum of all available item weights.

Chance = tier weight × category weight × item multiplier / sum of all weights

With T1 = 4 and T4 = 1, one particular T1 item is four times as likely as one T4 item. The overall tier chance also depends on the number of items in that tier.

Random values come from the Windows / Chromium cryptographic generator. The wheel animates an already chosen result; sector size represents its probability.

Drawn items stay excluded until a new session. Filters and weights do not return them to the pool. History and settings are saved automatically after each draw.

Set interface scale from 75% to 250% in the top bar; it is saved when you close the app. Ctrl + / Ctrl − zoom in and out, Ctrl 0 restores 100%. Ctrl + mouse wheel also works.

♪ plays Deadlock main menu music. The speaker button separately controls reel clicks and result sounds. Volume settings include an independent slider and mute switch for soft interface sounds. RU / EN changes the interface language and item names. All of these settings are saved automatically.
